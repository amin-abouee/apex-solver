# SE₂(3) — Extended Poses (Rotation + Position + Velocity)

$SE_2(3)$ is the group of **extended poses** — a rotation, a position, and a
velocity bundled into one Lie group. It has $\text{DOF} = 9$
($\text{REP\_SIZE} = 10$) and is the natural state for inertial navigation and
IMU pre-integration, where it gives clean, coupled Jacobians for orientation,
position and velocity.

$$
SE_2(3) = \left\{\, \mathbf{X} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{R} & \mathbf{v} & \mathbf{p} \\ \mathbf{0} & 1 & 0 \\ \mathbf{0} & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \;\middle|\; \mathbf{R} \in SO(3),\ \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^3 \,\right\} \subset \mathbb{R}^{5\times 5}.
$$

## Representation

| Storage | Layout |
|---|---|
| Group `SE23` | `SVector<f64, 10>` = $[\mathbf{t}, q_w, q_x, q_y, q_z, \mathbf{v}]$ (position, quaternion, velocity) |
| Tangent `SE23Tangent` | `Vector9<f64>` = $[\boldsymbol{\rho},\ \boldsymbol{\theta},\ \boldsymbol{\nu}]$ |
| Lie algebra | $5\times 5$ matrix (see below) |

Note the tangent order $[\boldsymbol{\rho}, \boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\nu}]$
(position, rotation, velocity) differs from the parameter storage order
(position, quaternion, velocity).

## Group Operations

| Operation | Formula | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | $\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{I}$, $\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$ | `SE23::identity()` |
| Composition | $\mathbf{X}_1 \mathbf{X}_2$ (matrix product) | `compose` |
| Inverse | $\big(\mathbf{R}^\top,\ -\mathbf{R}^\top\mathbf{p},\ -\mathbf{R}^\top\mathbf{v}\big)$ | `inverse` |
| Action | $\mathbf{R}\,\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{p}$ | `act` |

## Lie Algebra (Hat / Vee)

$$
\boldsymbol{\tau}^\wedge =
\begin{bmatrix} [\boldsymbol{\theta}]_\times & \boldsymbol{\nu} & \boldsymbol{\rho} \\ \mathbf{0} & 0 & 0 \\ \mathbf{0} & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{5\times 5},
\qquad
\boldsymbol{\tau} = (\boldsymbol{\rho}, \boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\nu}) \in \mathbb{R}^9 .
$$

<a id="exp"></a>
## Exponential Map

Rotation exponentiates as in SO(3); **both** the position and velocity are mapped
through the *same* left Jacobian $\mathbf{J}_l(\boldsymbol{\theta})$:

$$
\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol{\tau}) = \Big(\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol{\theta}),\ \ \mathbf{p} = \mathbf{J}_l(\boldsymbol{\theta})\,\boldsymbol{\rho},\ \ \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{J}_l(\boldsymbol{\theta})\,\boldsymbol{\nu}\Big).
$$

<a id="log"></a>
## Logarithmic Map

$$
\mathrm{Log}(\mathbf{X}) = \Big(\mathbf{J}_l^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\theta})\,\mathbf{p},\ \ \boldsymbol{\theta},\ \ \mathbf{J}_l^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\theta})\,\mathbf{v}\Big),
\qquad \boldsymbol{\theta} = \mathrm{Log}(\mathbf{R}).
$$

## Adjoint

$$
\mathrm{Ad}_{\mathbf{X}} =
\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{R} & [\mathbf{p}]_\times \mathbf{R} & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{R} & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & [\mathbf{v}]_\times \mathbf{R} & \mathbf{R} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{9\times 9}.
$$

<a id="jacobians"></a>
## Jacobians

The $9\times 9$ right Jacobian reuses the SE(3) coupling block
$\mathbf{Q}(\cdot, \boldsymbol{\theta})$ (see [SE(3)](./se3.md#jacobians)) once for
position and once for velocity:

$$
\mathbf{J}_r(\boldsymbol{\tau}) =
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{J}_r(\boldsymbol{\theta}) & \mathbf{Q}(\boldsymbol{\rho}, \boldsymbol{\theta}) & \mathbf{0} \\
\mathbf{0} & \mathbf{J}_r(\boldsymbol{\theta}) & \mathbf{0} \\
\mathbf{0} & \mathbf{Q}(\boldsymbol{\nu}, \boldsymbol{\theta}) & \mathbf{J}_r(\boldsymbol{\theta})
\end{bmatrix},
$$

with $\mathbf{Q}$ computed by `SE23Tangent::q_matrix`. Left Jacobian and inverses
follow the same pattern (`left_jacobian()`, `right_jacobian_inv()`, …).

## Plus and Minus

$$
\mathbf{X} \oplus \boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{X}\,\mathrm{Exp}(\boldsymbol{\tau}),
\qquad
\mathbf{X}_1 \ominus \mathbf{X}_2 = \mathrm{Log}\!\left(\mathbf{X}_2^{-1}\mathbf{X}_1\right).
$$

## Example

```rust
use apex_solver::manifold::se23::{SE23, SE23Tangent};
use apex_solver::manifold::{LieGroup, Tangent};
use nalgebra::{Vector3, UnitQuaternion};

let state = SE23::new(
    Vector3::new(1.0, 0.0, 0.0),   // position
    Vector3::new(0.0, 0.0, 9.8),   // velocity
    UnitQuaternion::identity(),
);
let delta = SE23Tangent::new(
    Vector3::new(0.01, 0.0, 0.0),  // ρ
    Vector3::new(0.0, 0.02, 0.0),  // θ
    Vector3::new(0.0, 0.0, 0.05),  // ν
);
let updated = state.plus(&delta, None, None);
assert!(state.is_approx(&state.log(None).exp(None), 1e-9));
```
