# Mathematical Cookbooks (workspace crates)

The deep mathematical derivations live with the crates they document, as
mdBook cookbooks rendered with KaTeX:

- **[apex-manifolds cookbook](https://github.com/amin-abouee/apex-solver/tree/main/crates/apex-manifolds/doc/cookbook/src/introduction.md)**
  — conventions, exponential/log maps, Jacobians and adjoints for
  SO(2), SO(3), SE(2), SE(3), SE₂(3), SGal(3), Sim(3) and ℝⁿ.
- **[apex-camera-models cookbook](https://github.com/amin-abouee/apex-solver/tree/main/crates/apex-camera-models/doc/cookbook/src/introduction.md)**
  — projection and unprojection derivations for the supported camera models.
- **[apex-io cookbook](https://github.com/amin-abouee/apex-solver/tree/main/crates/apex-io/doc/cookbook/src/introduction.md)**
  — file-format details for G2O, TORO and BAL.

The published site (this one) aggregates them:

- [apex-manifolds cookbook](/apex-manifolds/) *(after the Pages deployment)*
- [apex-camera-models cookbook](/apex-camera-models/)
- [apex-io cookbook](/apex-io/)
